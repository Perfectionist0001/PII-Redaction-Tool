## 6. Known Limitations

1. **Zero human-reviewed candidates**: All 63 reviewed decisions were produced by automated policy simulation. No independent human auditor has verified any annotation. Precision, Recall, and F1 cannot be treated as finalized benchmark metrics.

2. **3,444 unreviewed candidates**: Only 63 of 3,507 candidates (1.8%) were assessed. The remaining 3,444 are unknown — not confirmed as true negatives.

3. **Single-document scope**: All testing was performed on one document (`Red Herring Prospectus.docx`). Generalizability to other documents is not established.

4. **spaCy `en_core_web_sm` model constraints**: The small NER model occasionally misses complex multi-word corporate names in dense tabular or parenthetical contexts. This was the root cause of the CareEdge leak, now resolved by span-aware co-reference propagation.

5. **No `SSN`, `CREDIT_CARD`, `DOB`, `IP_ADDRESS` ground-truth instances**: These four categories have zero annotated examples in the provisional ground truth; correctness for them is validated only by unit tests.

6. **Redacted document not independently human-audited**: Validation was performed programmatically. The methodology is sound but is not a substitute for manual page-by-page inspection.
